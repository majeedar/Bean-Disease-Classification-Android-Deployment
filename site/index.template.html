How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android, or scan the QR code with your phone camera.
  2. When the download finishes, open the file beandoc.apk.
  3. If your phone says apps from this source are not allowed, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

To update, download and install the new version the same way. Your phone keeps the app; nothing is lost.

What it does

  • Take a photo of one bean leaf, or choose one from your gallery.
  • See if it looks like angular leaf spot, bean rust, or healthy.
  • Get short advice on what to do next.
  • Works fully offline. Photos never leave your phone. No account, no ads.
This app gives general guidance and can make mistakes. If many plants are affected, ask your local extension officer.
